# CAGED help

The CAGED system shows the same chord in five overlapping shapes — **C**, **A**, **G**, **E**, and **D** — moved up and down the neck. Pick a key and a chord type at the top of the page to see all five shapes at once.

Use the legend to show or hide individual shapes by clicking their letter, and turn on **Show triads** to highlight the root, third, and fifth within each shape.

Switch between **Chord** view (just the chord shapes) and **Scale** view (the shapes overlaid on the full scale) with the toggle button. Scale view needs a standard 6-string guitar tuning — the toggle is disabled otherwise.

Use the gear icon to change guitar type, tuning, or how the fretboard is drawn. Use the print icon to print the diagrams shown.
