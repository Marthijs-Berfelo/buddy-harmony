# CAGED help

The CAGED system shows the same chord in five overlapping shapes — **C**, **A**, **G**, **E**, and **D** — moved up and down the neck. Pick a key and a chord type at the top of the page to see all five shapes at once.

### Legend

- Click a shape's letter to show or hide it
- Turn on **Show triads** to highlight the root, third, and fifth within each shape

### Chord vs. scale view

Switch between two views with the toggle button:

- **Chord** — just the chord shapes
- **Scale** — the shapes overlaid on the full scale

Scale view needs a standard 6-string guitar tuning — the toggle is disabled otherwise.

### Other tools

- Gear icon — change guitar type, tuning, or how the fretboard is drawn
- Print icon — print the diagrams shown
