# Help

Buddy Harmony draws interactive guitar diagrams to help you learn chords, scales, and how they connect across the fretboard.

### Pages

- **Chords** — look up every fretboard position for a chord
- **Scales** — see a full scale mapped across the neck
- **C A G E D** — see a chord's five CAGED shapes, or switch to the full scale behind them

### Getting started

Open any page from the menu, then pick a key and a chord or scale at the top. Each page has its own help button with tips specific to that page.
